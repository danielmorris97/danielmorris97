Research

I study sleep, dreaming, lucid dreaming, and consciousness using behavioral and neuroscientific approaches.

My research explores how we can interact with the sleeping brain, influence ongoing dreams, and use lucid dreaming as a tool for studying cognition and consciousness.

I conduct my doctoral research in the Cognitive Neuroscience Laboratory at Northwestern University.

Research Areas

Selected areas of my current research.

Lucid Dreaming

Studying awareness, communication, cognition, and volitional control during ongoing dreams.

Dream Engineering

Investigating how sensory stimulation and waking experiences can influence dreams during sleep.

Dream Yoga & Contemplative Science

Exploring lucid dreaming and related contemplative practices through interdisciplinary research.

Clinical Applications

Examining how lucid dreaming and targeted interventions may be useful for nightmares and other sleep-related challenges.
